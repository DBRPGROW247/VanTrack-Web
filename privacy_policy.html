01 Statutory Foundation

Introduction & Statutory Mandate

VanTrack is committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of school students, parents, drivers, and fleet owners. This Privacy Policy sets forth how personal data, location telemetry, student records, and device identifiers are gathered, stored, processed, and safeguarded when you access or interact with the VanTrack mobile application, driver interface, fleet dashboard, and official web portals.

Our privacy infrastructure is engineered in compliance with:

  • The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of the Republic of India.
  • Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.
  • Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.
  • National Geospatial Policy, 2022 & guidelines issued by the Survey of India (SOI).
  • Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement & Data Safety Requirements governing background location tracking, account deletion, and child protection.
Core Safety Charter DPDP Rule 4

VanTrack operates on the fundamental principle that child physical safety and digital privacy are inseparable. We never monetize, rent, or sell location telemetry or personal records to advertisers, marketing brokers, or commercial third parties. Every byte of telemetry collected exists solely to ensure students arrive at school and return home safely.

02 Consent Framework

DPDP Act 2023 (Section 5) Explicit Consent Notice

Statutory Explicit Consent Notice (DPDP Act, 2023 Section 5):

By installing VanTrack, signing into your account, or checking the consent acknowledgment checkbox during registration, you grant voluntary, informed, specific, and unambiguous consent for VanTrack to process your personal data and telemetry strictly for school transit coordination, proximity alerts, and student attendance verification.

You have the statutory right under DPDP Act Section 6(4) to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent will disable active GPS telemetry broadcasts, route visibility, and attendance notifications, and you may permanently request account and data erasure via our dedicated web portal at index.html or inside the app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account.

04 Location Architecture

GPS Telemetry, Geofencing & Location Policy

VanTrack utilizes high-precision Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) hardware to provide real-time school van tracking. Our location architecture adheres to the highest industry standards for privacy, battery conservation, and data efficiency:

Shift-Restricted Broadcasting

Driver location telemetry is collected ONLY while a driver is actively logged into a shift (Morning Route or Afternoon Route). Location streaming ceases instantly the moment the driver taps "End Shift" or signs out.

Adaptive Telemetry Throttling

When moving, coordinates broadcast every 3 seconds (5m movement threshold). When stationary in traffic or at school gates, polling drops to 15 seconds, saving battery and data while maintaining sub-50ms latency.

Automated 2-Stage Geofencing

Calculated on-device and via cloud microservices:
• Stage 1 (Approaching): Dispatched when van is within 500 meters.
• Stage 2 (Arrival): Dispatched when van arrives within 80 meters.

Zero Parent Background Location Tracking Strict Privacy

Parents are never tracked in the background. Parent device location is accessed only on an ephemeral, foreground-only basis when pinning pickup/drop stops via the interactive map pin-selector. VanTrack never tracks parents while they commute, work, or engage in private activities.

05 Device Security

Device Hardware Permissions

VanTrack requests explicit runtime operating system permissions on Android and iOS devices strictly for operational purposes:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Required for high-precision GNSS positioning. Used by drivers during active shifts to stream coordinates, and by parents while pinning student stops.
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION (Drivers Only): Requested strictly for drivers so telemetry continues streaming if the driver receives a phone call, locks the screen, or runs Google Maps Navigation in the foreground. This permission is never requested or required for parents.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to dispatch Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) alerts for proximity warnings, van departures, delays, and child boarding confirmations.
  • CAMERA & READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: Requested strictly when a fleet owner or driver uploads vehicle photos, fitness certificates, or driving license documents for safety verification. We never inspect or access personal photo libraries.
  • CALL_PHONE / DIALER INTENT: Utilized strictly to launch the phone dialer with prefilled numbers so parents, drivers, and fleet owners can instantly communicate regarding transit emergencies.
06 Child Protection

Child Safety & Minors Protection Policy

Statutory Compliance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act, 2023:

VanTrack enforces rigorous legal and technical safeguards to protect children and minors whose transport is managed through the platform.

Our child protection safeguards encompass:

  • Verifiable Parental Consent: Student profiles are enrolled and maintained exclusively by verified parents or legal guardians. Children under the age of 18 cannot independently create accounts or broadcast data.
  • Zero Behavioral Profiling or Tracking: In accordance with Section 9(2) of the DPDP Act, VanTrack shall not undertake tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children under any circumstances.
  • No Harmful Processing: In accordance with Section 9(1) of the DPDP Act, no data processing that could cause any detrimental effect on the well-being of a child is permitted on the platform.
  • Strict Data Minimization: Minor data is strictly limited to first name, school, standard, stop coordinates, and attendance timestamps. No personal phone numbers, biometric scans, or social profiles of children are ever collected.
  • Parent-Driven Access Control: Only the assigned vehicle's driver and fleet owner can see the child's boarding status during active transit. Other parents in the van cannot view student records or stop details of other children.
07 Architectural Security

Role-Based Data Isolation & Row Level Security (RLS)

VanTrack implements rigorous multi-tenant data segregation powered by PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) and cryptographic JSON Web Token (JWT) verification:

Cryptographic Wall of Isolation Postgres RLS Active

Every database query executed against VanTrack's backend is verified at the database kernel level against the user's cryptographically signed identity:

  • Parent Boundary: Parents can query telemetry, stops, and driver contacts strictly and exclusively for vehicles their verified enrolled child is assigned to. Direct queries for other vehicles or children are blocked at the database engine level with HTTP 403 Forbidden.
  • Driver Boundary: Drivers can view student stops and attendance records strictly for students enrolled on their assigned active shift roster.
  • Fleet Owner Boundary: Fleet owners can manage vehicles, drivers, and enrollments strictly for their own registered fleet domain.
08 Commercial Boundary

Zero Transit Fee Mediation & Financial Privacy

VanTrack operates exclusively as a technology software service (SaaS) provider facilitating real-time telemetry, parent communication, and fleet safety management.

Zero Offline Fee Collection

VanTrack does not collect, mediate, escrow, or process offline student van fees, monthly tuition, or transportation payments. All transit payments are negotiated and settled directly between parents and fleet owners outside the app.

Zero Banking Credentials

VanTrack never asks for, records, stores, or accesses credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking passwords, or UPI MPINs on its servers.

App Store Billing Transparency

Any digital subscriptions or software seat licenses for fleet management are processed exclusively via official Google Play Billing or authorized PCI-DSS certified payment gateways in full compliance with RBI guidelines.

09 Vetting & Compliance

Driver KYC & Background Records Governance

The safety of school transit requires verified and accountable personnel. Driver data is handled under specialized legal frameworks:

  • Mandatory Driving License Verification: Driving license credentials submitted by drivers or fleet owners are validated against state transport transport authority requirements for commercial passenger transport.
  • Aadhaar Masking Protocols: In compliance with the Aadhaar Act, 2016 and UIDAI regulations, any Aadhaar cards uploaded for driver verification must have the first 8 digits masked. VanTrack permanently scrubs unmasked Aadhaar documents.
  • Document Encryption at Rest: Fleet documents, fitness certificates, and driver credentials stored in Supabase Storage are encrypted using AES-256 with restricted time-limited pre-signed URL access.

11 Processors

Third-Party Processors & Infrastructure Security

VanTrack partners exclusively with industry-leading, enterprise-grade cloud providers meeting ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and DPDP Act security benchmarks:

  • Google Maps SDK & Directions API: Provides high-definition satellite base maps, traffic overlays, reverse geocoding, and driver turn-by-turn navigation handoff. Usage is governed by Google Privacy Policy.

13 Statutory Rights

User Rights, DPDP Act Compliance & Account Deletion

Under Section 11, 12, and 13 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Google Play User Data policies, you hold complete sovereignty over your information:

Right to Access (Section 11)

You may request a complete summary of all personal data, enrolled student details, and attendance logs associated with your account.

Right to Correction (Section 12)

You can correct outdated phone numbers, adjust pickup/drop coordinates, or update student roster details at any time.

Right to Erasure (Section 12)

You hold the unfettered right to permanently delete your account and erase all stored records across all database clusters.

72-Hour Breach Notification

In the event of a verified data breach, VanTrack legally commits to notifying the Data Protection Board of India and affected users within 72 hours.

Official Account & Data Deletion Channels 4 Instant Methods
  • 1. In-App Direct Erasure: Navigate to Profile → Settings → Delete Account inside the VanTrack mobile application to immediately trigger permanent cryptographic deletion.
  • 2. Web Deletion Portal: Access our official standalone erasure portal at index.html to submit an erasure request from any web browser without needing the app installed.
  • 3. Direct Email Request: Email our dedicated Data Protection Officer at grievance@vantrack.in or hostelhome@gmail.com with the subject "VanTrack Account Deletion Request".
  • 4. Fleet Owner Disenrollment: Fleet owners can unassign drivers and remove student stops upon parental request, clearing active transit ties.
