02 Child Safety & Eligibility

2. Age Eligibility, Minors Policy & Child Protection

VanTrack operates with utmost vigilance concerning student welfare. The service is structured around adult governance:

  • Strict Prohibition on Minor Account Creation: Children and individuals under eighteen (18) years of age are strictly prohibited from creating accounts, holding login credentials, or operating user profiles on the Platform.
  • Sole Parental & Guardian Authority: VanTrack accounts are created and managed solely by verified adults: Parents, Legal Guardians, Verified Commercial Drivers, and School Van Fleet Owners/Operators.
  • Section 9 DPDP Act Compliance: In strict conformity with Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023, VanTrack expressly prohibits undertaking tracking or behavioral monitoring directed at children, nor does it serve targeted advertisements or profiling algorithms based on children's personal data.
  • Vehicle-Level Telemetry Only: GPS hardware tracking is applied strictly to the commercial transit vehicle / driver device during active school commute shifts. No personal devices belonging to children are ever tracked or paired.
Guardian Representation & Warranty
Parents and legal guardians warrant that they hold valid lawful custody over any enrolled student whose pickup/drop coordinates and transit stop records are configured in the application.

05 Technical Accuracy

5. Real-Time GPS Telemetry, Geofencing & Operational Accuracy

VanTrack provides real-time vehicle telemetry using device GNSS hardware and WebSocket streams to calculate vehicle positioning and proximity alerts:

  • Two-Stage Automated Proximity Alerts: The platform dispatches notifications when the van is approaching (approximately ≤ 500 meters) and upon arrival (approximately ≤ 80 meters).
  • Algorithmic Approximations: Distances, estimated arrival times (ETAs), and geofences are mathematical computational estimates. They are subject to factors beyond VanTrack's control, including urban high-rise signal reflection ("urban canyons"), tunnels, severe weather, device battery optimization, and mobile telecom latency.
  • Navigation Handoff: Drivers utilizing the turn-by-turn navigation shortcut are handed off to their native Google Maps application. VanTrack is not liable for third-party navigation routing, traffic conditions, or map inaccuracies.
  • Complementary Tool Only: Parents and drivers agree that automated proximity alerts are assistive convenience utilities and must never replace human judgment, road vigilance, or proactive safety planning.

08 Identity & KYC Verification

8. Driver KYC, Identity Documents & Masked Aadhaar Policy

To verify driver authenticity and preserve passenger safety, VanTrack implements strict KYC document handling in conformity with the Supreme Court of India Puttaswamy verdict, UIDAI Regulations, and Google Play User Data policies:

  • Non-Mandatory Aadhaar Rule: Providing an Aadhaar card is voluntary. Drivers and operators are entitled to submit alternative Official Valid Documents (OVDs), including valid Commercial Driving Licenses, Voter Identity Cards, or Passports without denial of service.
  • Mandatory 8-Digit Masking: If an Aadhaar copy is uploaded, the driver/operator must ensure the first eight (8) digits are masked or redacted, displaying strictly the last four (4) digits, in accordance with UIDAI regulations.
  • Secure Ephemeral Storage: KYC identity images are stored in restricted, encrypted cloud storage buckets, accessed exclusively for authorized fleet compliance verification, and are never exposed publicly or commercialized.
